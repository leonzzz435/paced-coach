# Pre-Delivery Checklist

Run this before finalizing a design-heavy task.

## Direction

- The dominant direction is named and visible in the output.
- The page or reel feels intentional rather than generic.
- There is one primary focal point per viewport or scene.

## Product UI

- Mobile and desktop both work.
- Focus, hover, disabled, and loading states are coherent where applicable.
- Contrast is acceptable and the CTA is obvious.
- Motion is optional and restrained unless it teaches state or sequence.
- The result still belongs to the product, not a separate marketing microsite.

## Dashboard UI

- The primary question is answerable in one glance.
- Chart choice matches the comparison or trend being shown.
- Units, time windows, and deltas are visible.
- Secondary metrics do not outrank the main decision signal.

## Final pass

- Remove any flourish that does not improve clarity, trust, or story momentum.
